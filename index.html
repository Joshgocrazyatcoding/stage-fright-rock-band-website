Who are Stage Fright?

Stage Fright tells the story of a band formed by four misfits—shy guitarist Leo, fearless drummer Riley, skeptical bassist Max, and bold singer Nina—who each struggle with stage fright in their own way. Their first rehearsal is a chaotic disaster, and their debut gig at a local bar goes even worse, filled with mistakes and technical mishaps. But instead of being ridiculed, they win over the audience with their raw authenticity and determination. As they continue to play together, they grow not just as musicians but as individuals, learning to embrace their fears and imperfections. Their music—a gritty blend of grunge, punk, and alt-rock—becomes a reflection of their struggles and triumphs, proving that fear isn’t a barrier but a catalyst for something powerful. Stage Fright transforms their vulnerabilities into the heart of their sound, finding success in their honesty and resilience.
